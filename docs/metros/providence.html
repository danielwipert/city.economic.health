Economic Analysis

The Providence-Warwick metro area has an overall grade of C, ranking 36.7th percentile out of 50 US metros, with a labor demand composite score of 5.11. This city's economic character is most defined by its low unemployment rate of 4.30% and its high cost of living, with a PSF to wages ratio of 10.15. The combination of these metrics suggests a challenging environment for businesses looking to hire and retain talent.

Labor Demand

The employment growth rate in Providence-Warwick is +0.60% year-over-year, while weekly hours are deviating -0.025% from the city's own 12-month baseline. This combination signals a moderate expansion of labor demand, with some genuine demand for jobs, but not at a rate that is significantly above trend. The labor demand composite score of 5.11 is near the median, indicating a stable but not exceptional job market.

Unemployment

The unemployment rate in Providence-Warwick is 4.30%, which is below average, ranking in the 20th percentile. This indicates a relatively tight labor market, making it harder for businesses to hire and potentially leading to upward pressure on wages. Companies looking to staff up in this city may face challenges in finding available workers.

Wage Growth

The year-over-year wage growth in Providence-Warwick is +3.00%, which is near the median, ranking in the 40th percentile. This moderate wage growth rate suggests that labor costs for employers are rising, but not at an alarming rate, while workers are seeing some improvement in their purchasing power. However, the wage growth is not strong enough to drive significant economic expansion.

Cost of Living

Providence-Warwick has a cost of living percentile rank of 2, indicating that it is one of the more expensive cities in the US, with a PSF to wages ratio of 10.15 and a year-over-year increase in PSF of 8.9%. This means that the city is relatively unaffordable, making it challenging to attract talent without offering wage premiums. The high cost of living may be a significant deterrent for businesses looking to relocate or expand in this city.

Labor Force Growth

The civilian labor force in Providence-Warwick is growing at a rate of +1.69% year-over-year, which is in the top tier, ranking in the 88th percentile. This indicates that the workforce supply is expanding, providing a positive environment for hiring and business growth. Companies looking to staff up in this city may find a growing pool of available workers.

Building Permits

The year-over-year change in building permits in Providence-Warwick is -8.13%, which is below average, ranking in the 34th percentile. This decline in building permits suggests that the housing supply is tightening, which may lead to future affordability issues and challenges in accommodating a growing workforce. This could be a concern for businesses looking to attract and retain talent in the city.

Days on Market

The median days on market in Providence-Warwick is 32 days, with a year-over-year increase of 3.2%. This indicates a relatively slow market, ranking in the 14th percentile, making it more accessible for workers relocating to the city. However, the rising days on market may also signal a normalization of the housing market, rather than a significant decline in demand.

Office Economy

Providence-Warwick has an office economy percentile rank of 68, indicating a deep professional talent pool, with 3.10 out of 5 composite score. This makes the city well-suited for businesses in the tech, finance, consulting, and HQ sectors, but less suitable for industrial or logistics-dominant economies. The city's professional workforce is a significant advantage for businesses looking to tap into a knowledgeable and skilled labor pool.

The Providence-Warwick metro area offers a growing labor force and a deep professional talent pool, making it an attractive location for businesses in certain sectors. However, the city's high cost of living and tightening housing supply are significant concerns that may impact businesses' ability to attract and retain talent, and should be carefully considered by decision-makers.
