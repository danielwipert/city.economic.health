Economic Analysis

The San Antonio-New Braunfels metro area has an overall grade of B- with a composite score ranking it at the 51.2th percentile among 50 US metros. This city's economic character is most defined by its below-average labor demand, with a composite score of 4.36, and its highly affordable cost of living, ranked at the 92nd percentile. The cost of living is particularly notable, with a price per square foot of $170 and average hourly earnings of $33.17, resulting in a ratio of 5.13.

Labor Demand

The employment growth rate in San Antonio-New Braunfels is 0.51% year-over-year, while weekly hours are deviating -0.651% from the city's own 12-month baseline. This combination signals a lack of genuine demand expansion, as hours are not running above trend during a period of modest job growth. The labor demand composite score of 4.36 is below average, ranking at the 28th percentile.

Unemployment

The unemployment rate in San Antonio-New Braunfels is 4.50%, which is below average, ranking at the 30th percentile. This indicates a relatively tight labor market, although not extremely so. For a business trying to hire in this city, the moderate unemployment rate suggests some competition for talent, but not an overly challenging hiring environment.

Wage Growth

The year-over-year wage growth rate in San Antonio-New Braunfels is 3.73%, which is near the median, ranking at the 50th percentile. This moderate wage growth rate suggests that labor costs for employers are rising, but not extremely quickly, while worker purchasing power is increasing at a steady pace. The wage growth rate is neither exceptionally fast nor stagnant, providing a relatively stable environment for businesses.

Cost of Living

San Antonio-New Braunfels has a highly affordable cost of living, with a percentile rank of 92. The price per square foot is $170, and average hourly earnings are $33.17, resulting in a ratio of 5.13. This affordability is a significant advantage for talent attraction, as businesses can offer competitive wages without needing to provide large premiums to offset high living costs.

Labor Force Growth

The civilian labor force in San Antonio-New Braunfels is growing at a rate of 0.23% year-over-year, which is above average, ranking at the 68th percentile. This expanding labor force supply suggests that the city has a growing pool of potential workers, making it easier for businesses to hire and expand their operations.

Building Permits

The number of residential building permits in San Antonio-New Braunfels is increasing by 25.98% year-over-year, which is above average, ranking at the 76th percentile. This rapid growth in building permits signals that housing supply is expanding, which should improve affordability and accommodate a growing workforce in the future.

Days on Market

The median days on market for homes in San Antonio-New Braunfels is 71 days, with a year-over-year decrease of 2.7%. This relatively fast market suggests that workers relocating to the city may face competition for housing, although the market is not extremely heated. The decreasing days on market indicates a strong demand for housing, which may pose challenges for relocating workers.

Office Economy

San Antonio-New Braunfels has a deep professional talent pool, with an office economy percentile rank of 76. This suggests that the city is well-suited for businesses in the tech, finance, consulting, and HQ sectors, which require a high concentration of specialized knowledge workers. However, the city may be less suitable for industries with primarily industrial or logistics-based workforces.

The San Antonio-New Braunfels metro area offers businesses a unique combination of affordability, moderate wage growth, and an expanding labor force. However, the single biggest risk or constraint for decision-makers is the below-average labor demand, which may limit the city's ability to support extremely rapid business growth or expansion. Despite this, the city's overall economic character and highly affordable cost of living make it an attractive location for businesses seeking a stable and competitive environment.
