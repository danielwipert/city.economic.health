Economic Analysis

The San Antonio-New Braunfels metro area has an overall grade of B- with a composite score ranking it at the 51.2th percentile among 50 US metros. This city's economic character is most defined by its below-average labor demand, with a composite score of 4.36, and its highly affordable cost of living, ranked at the 92nd percentile. The cost of living is particularly notable, with a price per square foot of $170 and average hourly earnings of $33.17, resulting in a ratio of 5.13.

Labor Demand

The employment growth rate in San Antonio-New Braunfels is 0.51% year-over-year, while weekly hours are deviating -0.651% from the city's own 12-month baseline. This combination signals a lack of genuine demand expansion, as hours are not running above trend during a period of job growth. Instead, it may indicate a moderate pace of job additions without significant pressure on labor resources.

Unemployment

The unemployment rate in San Antonio-New Braunfels is 4.50%, which is below average, ranking at the 30th percentile. This indicates a relatively tight labor market with some slack, making it moderately challenging for businesses to hire. However, it is not as competitive as other cities with lower unemployment rates.

Wage Growth

The year-over-year wage growth in San Antonio-New Braunfels is 3.73%, which is near the median, ranking at the 50th percentile. This moderate wage growth rate suggests that labor costs for employers are rising at a steady pace, while worker purchasing power is increasing, but not rapidly. This balance may appeal to businesses seeking to manage labor costs without sacrificing worker satisfaction.

Cost of Living

San Antonio-New Braunfels has a highly affordable cost of living, ranked at the 92nd percentile, with a price per square foot of $170 and average hourly earnings of $33.17. This affordability advantage is driven by a -3.4% year-over-year decrease in price per square foot. As a result, the city offers a significant talent attraction advantage without requiring wage premiums to compensate for high living costs.

Labor Force Growth

The civilian labor force in San Antonio-New Braunfels is growing at a rate of 0.23% year-over-year, which is above average, ranking at the 68th percentile. This expansion of the labor force supply indicates a positive trend for hiring capacity, as the pool of potential workers is increasing.

Building Permits

The number of residential building permits in San Antonio-New Braunfels has increased by 25.98% year-over-year, which is above average, ranking at the 76th percentile. This significant expansion of housing supply suggests that future affordability and workforce accommodation are likely to improve, reducing the risk of a supply squeeze.

Days on Market

The current median days on market in San Antonio-New Braunfels is 71 days, with a year-over-year decrease of -2.7%. This relatively fast pace of home sales indicates a competitive market, which may pose challenges for relocating workers. However, the decreasing trend in days on market suggests a normalization of the housing market.

Office Economy

San Antonio-New Braunfels has a deep professional talent pool, ranked at the 76th percentile, with a share of jobs in professional and office sectors. This makes the city well-suited for businesses in the tech, finance, consulting, and HQ sectors, but less ideal for industrial or logistics-dominant economies.

The San Antonio-New Braunfels metro area offers businesses a unique combination of affordable living costs, moderate wage growth, and an expanding labor force. However, the single biggest risk or constraint for decision-makers is the below-average labor demand, which may limit the city's ability to support rapid business growth or expansion.
