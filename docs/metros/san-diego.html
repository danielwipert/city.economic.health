Economic Analysis

The San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad metro area has an overall grade of C, ranking 37.5th percentile out of 50 US metros, with a composite score driven largely by its strong wage growth and office economy. The city's economic character is most defined by its top-tier wage growth of 6.72% year-over-year and its below-average labor demand composite score of 4.53. These metrics signal a complex labor market with both positive and negative trends.

Labor Demand

The employment growth rate in San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad is 0.51% year-over-year, while weekly hours are deviating -0.482% from the city's own 12-month baseline. This combination signals a labor market with some job growth, but hours are not expanding as quickly as might be expected, indicating a potential survivor squeeze where remaining workers are absorbing the load of eliminated roles. This suggests that while there is some demand for labor, it is not as strong as it could be.

Unemployment

The unemployment rate in San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad is 4.70%, which is near the median, ranking 42nd percentile. This indicates a labor market with some slack, making it slightly easier for businesses to hire compared to very tight markets. However, it also means that local consumer demand may not be as strong as in areas with lower unemployment rates.

Wage Growth

The year-over-year wage growth in San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad is 6.72%, which is in the top tier, ranking 92nd percentile. This fast wage growth implies rising labor costs for employers, but it also means good news for worker purchasing power. Businesses considering locating in this area should be prepared for a competitive labor market where wages are increasing rapidly.

Cost of Living

San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad has a cost of living that is relatively expensive, with a percentile rank of 12th, indicating that it is in the bottom tier for affordability. The city's PSF is $594/sqft, which is decreasing by 2.8% year-over-year, but the ratio of PSF to hourly earnings of $43.84/hr is still 13.55. This suggests that the city may struggle to attract talent without offering wage premiums to offset the high cost of living.

Labor Force Growth

The civilian labor force in San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad is contracting at a rate of -1.75% year-over-year, which is in the bottom tier, ranking 12th percentile. This indicates that the labor force supply is shrinking, presenting a structural headwind for hiring and potentially limiting the city's ability to support business growth.

Building Permits

The number of building permits in San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad is decreasing by 37.09% year-over-year, which is in the bottom tier, ranking 4th percentile. This sharp decline in permits suggests that housing supply is tightening, which could lead to future affordability issues and challenges in accommodating a growing workforce.

Days on Market

The median days on market for homes in San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad is 45 days, with no year-over-year change. This indicates a relatively stable housing market that is neither extremely competitive nor very accessible for relocating workers. However, the lack of change in days on market, combined with the city's other labor market trends, suggests that the housing market may not be a major constraint for businesses locating in the area.

Office Economy

San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad has an above-average share of professional and office workers, ranking 60th percentile. This indicates a deep talent pool suited for businesses in the tech, finance, consulting, and HQ sectors. However, the city may be less suited for industries with more industrial or logistics-dominant economies.

The San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad metro area offers businesses a unique combination of strong wage growth and a deep professional talent pool, making it an attractive location for certain types of companies. However, the city's high cost of living, shrinking labor force, and tightening housing supply present significant risks and constraints that decision-makers should carefully consider when evaluating this location for their business.
