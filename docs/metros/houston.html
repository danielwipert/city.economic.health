Economic Analysis

The Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro area has an overall grade of B- with a composite score ranking it at the 49.2th percentile out of 50 US metros. This city's economic character is most defined by its high cost of living affordability, with a percentile rank of 96th, and its below-average wage growth rate of 1.71% year-over-year. The labor demand composite score of 5.71, ranking at the 68th percentile, also plays a significant role in shaping the city's economic landscape.

Labor Demand

The employment growth rate in Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands is 1.46% year-over-year, combined with a weekly hours deviation of -0.639% from its own trend. This combination signals a genuine demand expansion, as the city is adding jobs and hours are running above trend. The labor demand composite score of 5.71, ranking at the 68th percentile, further supports this indication of a growing labor market.

Unemployment

The unemployment rate in Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands is 5.10%, ranking at the 8th percentile, indicating a relatively high level of unemployment. This means the market has some slack, making it easier for businesses to hire workers. However, this also implies weaker local consumer demand, which could impact businesses reliant on local spending.

Wage Growth

The year-over-year wage growth rate in Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands is 1.71%, ranking at the 22nd percentile, indicating stagnant wage growth. This slow wage growth means labor costs for employers are not rising rapidly, but it also translates to weak bargaining power for workers and potentially limited purchasing power.

Cost of Living

Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands has a cost of living percentile rank of 96th, with a price-to-salary ratio of $169/sqft to $36.90/hr, or 4.58. This high rank indicates that the city is more affordable relative to its peers, making it an attractive location for talent without requiring significant wage premiums. The fact that PSF is falling by 2.3% year-over-year further enhances the city's affordability.

Labor Force Growth

The civilian labor force in Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands is growing at a rate of 0.28% year-over-year, ranking at the 64th percentile. This positive growth rate indicates that the workforce supply is expanding, which is a favorable condition for businesses looking to hire. However, the growth rate is relatively modest, suggesting that hiring capacity, while present, is not rapidly increasing.

Building Permits

The year-over-year change in residential building permits in Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands is -6.61%, ranking near the median at the 42nd percentile. This decline suggests that housing supply is tightening, which could lead to future affordability issues and challenges in accommodating a growing workforce.

Days on Market

The median days on market for homes in Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands is 52 days, with a year-over-year increase of 2.0%. This indicates a relatively stable market that is not overly competitive, making it accessible for workers relocating to the city. The near-median percentile rank of 56th further supports this assessment, suggesting a balanced market.

Office Economy

Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands has an office economy percentile rank of 78th, indicating a deep talent pool in professional and office sectors. This makes the city well-suited for businesses in tech, finance, consulting, and HQ decisions that require specialized knowledge workers. However, it may be less ideal for industries dominated by industrial or logistics roles.

The Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro area offers businesses a unique combination of affordability and access to a professional talent pool, but the single biggest risk or constraint is the stagnant wage growth, which could limit worker purchasing power and bargaining ability. Despite this, the city's overall economic landscape, particularly its high affordability and expanding labor force, presents opportunities for businesses looking to establish or expand operations in a relatively accessible market.
