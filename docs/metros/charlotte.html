Economic Analysis

The Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia metro area has earned an overall grade of A- with a composite score ranking in the 67.9th percentile among 50 US metros. This city's economic character is most defined by its strong labor demand, with a top-tier composite score of 96th percentile, and its relatively low unemployment rate of 3.70%, ranking in the 84th percentile. The combination of these metrics suggests a city with a robust job market and limited labor supply slack.

Labor Demand

The Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia metro area has seen employment growth of +1.54% year-over-year, combined with a +1.361% deviation in weekly hours above its own 12-month trend. This signals genuine demand expansion, as hours are running above trend during a period of job growth, indicating that the labor market is experiencing an increase in workload without relying on survivor squeeze. The labor demand composite score of 7.82 further reinforces this notion, ranking in the top tier at 96th percentile.

Unemployment

The unemployment rate in Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia stands at 3.70%, which is relatively low and ranks in the 84th percentile. This indicates a tight labor market with limited slack, making it more challenging for businesses to hire new employees. As a result, companies may face upward pressure on wages to attract and retain top talent in this competitive job market.

Wage Growth

The year-over-year wage growth in Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia is +4.22%, ranking in the 62nd percentile. This moderate wage growth rate suggests that labor costs for employers are rising, but not excessively so. At the same time, workers are experiencing an increase in purchasing power, which can have a positive impact on local consumer demand.

Cost of Living

With a cost of living ratio of $217/sqft to $38.91/hr, Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia ranks in the 77th percentile in terms of affordability. The fact that PSF is decreasing by -1.4% year-over-year relative to wages makes the city more attractive for talent, as it offers a relatively affordable environment without requiring significant wage premiums. This can be a major advantage for businesses looking to attract and retain employees.

Labor Force Growth

The civilian labor force in Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia has contracted by -1.49% year-over-year, ranking near the median at 40th percentile. This decline in labor force supply poses a structural headwind for hiring, as businesses may face challenges in finding qualified candidates to fill open positions.

Building Permits

The number of residential building permits in Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia has decreased by -22.75% year-over-year, ranking in the bottom tier at 12th percentile. This sharp decline in permits suggests that housing supply is tightening, which may lead to future affordability issues and challenges in accommodating a growing workforce.

Days on Market

The current median days on market in Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia is 64 days, with a year-over-year increase of +10.3%. This indicates a slower market, ranking in the bottom tier at 16th percentile. For workers relocating to this city, the slower market may make it more accessible to find housing, but it also signals a potential erosion of demand.

Office Economy

Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia has a deep professional talent pool, with an office/professional worker share ranking in the top tier at 86th percentile. This makes the city well-suited for businesses in the tech, finance, consulting, and HQ sectors, which rely on a knowledgeable and specialized workforce. In contrast, it may be less suitable for industries with more industrial or logistics-oriented economies.

The Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia metro area offers businesses a strong labor market with genuine demand expansion, a relatively low unemployment rate, and a moderate wage growth environment. However, the single biggest risk or constraint for decision-makers is the tightening housing supply, which may lead to future affordability issues and challenges in accommodating a growing workforce, potentially offsetting the city's otherwise attractive economic characteristics.
