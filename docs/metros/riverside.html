Economic Analysis

The Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario metro area has an overall grade of C+ with a composite score ranking it 38.6th percentile out of 50 US metros. This city's economic character is most defined by its below-average labor demand, with an employment growth rate of +0.79% and weekly hours deviation of -0.845%, and its strong wage growth of +6.02% year-over-year. These metrics signal a mixed economic environment, with some positive trends but also areas of concern.

Labor Demand

The employment growth rate of +0.79% and weekly hours deviation of -0.845% combine to signal a labor market that is not experiencing genuine demand expansion. This suggests that while some jobs are being added, the hours worked are below trend, indicating a potential survivor squeeze where remaining workers are absorbing the load of eliminated roles. This mixed signal warrants caution for businesses considering location decisions.

Unemployment

The unemployment rate of 5.30% indicates a labor market with some slack, ranking below average at the 32nd percentile. This means that businesses may find it relatively easier to hire in this market, but may also face weaker local consumer demand. The practical implication is that companies may need to balance the benefits of a slightly looser labor market with the potential drawbacks of lower consumer spending power.

Wage Growth

The year-over-year wage growth rate of +6.02% is strong, ranking in the top tier at the 88th percentile. This signals rising labor costs for employers, but also good news for worker purchasing power. As wages continue to grow, businesses may need to factor in increasing labor costs, but may also benefit from a more affluent local consumer base.

Cost of Living

The cost of living in Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario is below average, with a percentile rank of 35th, and a PSF to earnings ratio of $333/sqft to $35.60/hr, or 9.35. The fact that PSF is falling -1.8% year-over-year is a key driver of this affordability score. This means that the city may have a talent attraction advantage, as workers may be drawn to the area's relatively lower cost of living without requiring wage premiums.

Labor Force Growth

The civilian labor force is contracting at a rate of -1.87% year-over-year, ranking in the bottom tier at the 8th percentile. This signals a shrinking labor pool, which may pose a structural headwind for hiring in the future. Businesses may need to consider strategies to attract and retain talent in a market with a declining labor force.

Building Permits

The year-over-year change in building permits is -13.30%, indicating a tightening of housing supply. This signals a potential future affordability challenge and may make it more difficult for workers to relocate to the area. As housing supply continues to contract, businesses may need to factor in the potential impacts on workforce accommodation and talent attraction.

Days on Market

The current median days on market is 61 days, with a year-over-year direction of -1.6%. This suggests a relatively fast-moving market, which may make it challenging for relocating workers to find housing. However, the slight decrease in days on market may indicate a healthy normalization of the market, rather than a demand erosion.

Office Economy

The professional talent pool in Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario is relatively shallow, ranking in the bottom tier at the 0th percentile. This means that the city may be less suited for businesses that require a deep knowledge-economy talent pool, such as tech or finance companies. However, the area may be more attractive to industries with fewer specialized roles, such as logistics or manufacturing.

The Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario metro area offers a mixed economic environment, with strong wage growth but also concerns around labor demand and labor force contraction. The single biggest risk or constraint for businesses considering this location is the potential future affordability challenge posed by a tightening housing supply, which may impact workforce accommodation and talent attraction.
