Economic Analysis

The Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario metro area has an overall grade of C, ranking 34.2th percentile out of 50 US metros, with a labor demand composite score of 5.20. This city's economic character is most defined by its high wage growth rate of +5.51% year-over-year and its low unemployment rate of 5.70%, which signals a tight labor market. The combination of these metrics suggests a challenging hiring environment for businesses.

Labor Demand

The employment growth rate in Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario is +0.75% year-over-year, while weekly hours are deviating -0.149% from the city's own 12-month baseline. This combination signals a moderate labor demand expansion, with some genuine demand for jobs but not excessively high. The labor demand composite score of 5.20 is near the median, indicating a balanced job market.

Unemployment

The unemployment rate in Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario is 5.70%, which is relatively high and ranks in the bottom tier at the 14th percentile. This suggests that the labor market has some slack, making it easier for businesses to hire workers. However, the practical implication is that local consumer demand may be weaker due to the higher unemployment rate.

Wage Growth

The year-over-year wage growth rate in Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario is +5.51%, which is above average and ranks in the 78th percentile. This indicates fast wage growth, which will lead to rising labor costs for employers but also increase worker purchasing power. Businesses should factor in higher labor costs when considering this location.

Cost of Living

Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario has a cost of living ratio of $332/sqft to $35.45/hr, with a percentile rank of 29th, indicating that the city is relatively expensive. The PSF is falling -1.2% year-over-year, but this is not enough to offset the high cost of living. This means that businesses may need to offer wage premiums to attract talent, as the city's affordability is below average.

Labor Force Growth

The civilian labor force in Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario is contracting at a rate of -1.75% year-over-year, which is in the bottom tier at the 12th percentile. This indicates that the labor force supply is shrinking, creating a structural headwind for hiring and business expansion. Businesses should be cautious when considering this location due to the limited labor pool.

Building Permits

The number of building permits in Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario is falling -19.99% year-over-year, which is in the bottom tier at the 14th percentile. This suggests that housing supply is tightening, which may lead to future affordability issues and challenges in accommodating a growing workforce. Businesses should consider the potential impact on employee relocation and retention.

Days on Market

The median days on market in Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario is 63 days, with a year-over-year decrease of -4.5%. This indicates a relatively fast-paced market, making it challenging for relocating workers to find housing. The near-median percentile rank of 54th suggests that the market is competitive, but not excessively so.

Office Economy

Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario has a very low share of professional and office workers, ranking in the 0th percentile. This indicates a shallow talent pool for businesses requiring specialized knowledge-economy workers, making it less suitable for tech, finance, or consulting companies. However, it may be more suitable for industrial or logistics-dominant businesses.

The Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario metro area offers businesses a unique combination of high wage growth and a relatively tight labor market, but the single biggest risk or constraint is the shrinking labor force supply and tightening housing market. Decision-makers should carefully consider these factors when evaluating this location for business expansion or relocation, as they may impact hiring capacity and employee retention. Overall, the city's economic character is complex, and businesses should weigh the pros and cons carefully before making a decision.
