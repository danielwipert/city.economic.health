# Memphis

**Grade: B- (Below Average) | 54.9th percentile | November 2026**

---

Memphis has a B- overall grade. The city's economy is defined by strong hiring and affordable housing, with employment growth at 0.2% over the past year.

**Labor Demand**
The city is adding jobs, with 0.2% growth over the past year. Workers' hours are rising, indicating genuine demand.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 4.1%. This makes it about average for employers to hire.

**Wage Growth**
Pay rose 3.6% over the past year. This means moderate cost increases for employers and decent pay raises for workers.

**Cost of Living**
Housing is affordable, with $152 per square foot and $33.13 hourly pay. Prices are falling 1.9% from last year, making it even more affordable.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce is shrinking, with -1.7% growth over the past year. This makes hiring more challenging.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals rose 113.0% over the past year. This signals a strong future housing supply.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 73 days to sell, with a 7.3% increase from last year. This indicates a softer housing market.

**Office Economy**
The office-based workforce is small. This suits industrial or logistics businesses better than tech or finance.

The city's biggest strength is its affordable housing. Its biggest risk is a shrinking workforce, making hiring more difficult.
