# Cleveland

**Grade: B- (Below Average) | 52.6th percentile | November 2026**

---

Cleveland earns a B- grade. The city's economy is defined by slow wage growth, at +1.8% over the past year.

**Labor Demand**
The city added 0.6% more jobs over the past year. Workers' hours rose 0.6%, indicating genuine demand.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 3.4%. This makes it about average for employers to hire.

**Wage Growth**
Pay rose 1.8% over the past year. This slow growth means lower labor costs for employers.

**Cost of Living**
Housing costs $143 per square foot, up 13.5% from a year ago. This is about average for affordability.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce grew 1.8% over the past year. This fast growth helps hiring.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals fell 6.8% over the past year. This signals a potential future housing supply squeeze.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 55 days to sell, up 7.8% from a year ago. This is a healthier housing market.

**Office Economy**
The city has a large office-based workforce. This suits tech, finance, and professional services businesses.

The city's biggest strength is its fast labor force growth. Its biggest risk is slow wage growth, affecting worker purchasing power.
