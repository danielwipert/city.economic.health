# Tampa-St. Petersburg-Clearwater

**Grade: B- (Below Average) | 54.3th percentile | November 2026**

---

The Tampa-St. Petersburg-Clearwater metro area has a below-average overall grade of B-. The city's economy is defined by its relatively high unemployment rate of 4.3% and its growing labor force. The cost of living is a notable strength, with homes costing $232 per square foot, down 5.7% from last year.

**Labor Demand**
The city's job growth is 1.1% over the past year, with weekly hours falling 0.2%. This means employers are not seeing strong demand for labor.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 4.3%, higher than most metros. This makes it easier for businesses to hire, but also indicates weaker local consumer demand.

**Wage Growth**
Pay is growing 4.0% over the past year, about average for most metros. This means employers face moderate labor cost increases, while workers see decent pay raises.

**Cost of Living**
Homes cost 6.3 hours of local pay per square foot, with prices down 5.7% from last year. This makes the city more attractive for workers, with a lower cost of living than most metros.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce is growing 0.8% over the past year, faster than most metros. This means employers have a growing pool of potential hires.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals are up 3.7% over the past year. This signals a moderate increase in future housing supply, which could help keep prices in check.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 77 days to sell, down 2.5% from last year. This indicates a relatively healthy housing market, with homes selling at a moderate pace.

**Office Economy**
The city has a large office-based workforce, better than most metros. This makes it well-suited for tech, finance, and professional services businesses.

The city's biggest strength is its affordable cost of living, making it attractive for workers. However, its high unemployment rate poses a risk, indicating weaker local consumer demand and potential challenges for businesses relying on local sales.
