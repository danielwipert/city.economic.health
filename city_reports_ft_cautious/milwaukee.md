# Milwaukee-Waukesha

**Grade: B- (Below Average) | 44.3th percentile | August 2026**

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate in Milwaukee-Waukesha is 3.80%, which is relatively low and ranks in the 68th percentile. This suggests a tight labor market with some slack, making it moderately challenging for businesses to hire new employees. As a result, businesses may face upward pressure on wages to attract and retain top talent in this market.

Milwaukee-Waukesha has an office economy score that ranks in the 30th percentile, indicating a relatively shallow professional talent pool. The Milwaukee-Waukesha metro area offers businesses a mix of challenges and opportunities, with a growing labor force and expanding housing supply. The single biggest constraint for decision-makers is the city's tight labor market and stagnant wage growth, which may limit the availability of skilled workers and drive up labor costs.
