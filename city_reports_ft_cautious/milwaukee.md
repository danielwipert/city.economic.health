# Milwaukee-Waukesha

**Grade: C- (Critical) | 34.9th percentile | November 2026**

---

The city earns a C- grade. Milwaukee's economy is defined by slow job growth, with only 0.2% more jobs over the past year.

**Labor Demand**
The city added few jobs. Workers' hours fell 1.9% over the past year.

**Unemployment**
Unemployment is 3.2%. This makes hiring harder for employers.

**Wage Growth**
Pay rose only 0.2% slower. This means lower labor costs for employers.

**Cost of Living**
Housing costs $227 per square foot. This is less affordable than most metros.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce grew 0.4%. This helps hiring in the city.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals fell 4.2%. This signals a future housing supply squeeze.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 37 days to sell. This is a healthier housing market.

**Office Economy**
The office workforce is average. This suits general business needs.

The city's biggest strength is its low unemployment. Its biggest risk is slow job growth.
