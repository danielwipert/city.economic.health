# Milwaukee-Waukesha

**Grade: C- (Critical) | 34.9th percentile | November 2026**

---

The city of Milwaukee-Waukesha has an overall grade of C-, with a score of 34.9 out of 100. This city's economy is currently defined by slow job growth, with only 0.2% employment growth over the past year. The unemployment rate is 3.2%, which is lower than most metros.

**Labor Demand**
The city has 0.2% job growth and -1.9% weekly hours, weaker than most metros. This means people looking for work may struggle to find jobs with good hours.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 3.2%, lower than most metros. This makes hiring harder for employers, as there are fewer available workers.

**Wage Growth**
Pay growth is -0.2% over the past year, slower than most metros. This means employers have lower labor costs, but workers have less bargaining power.

**Cost of Living**
It takes 6.3 hours of pay to buy one square foot of home, with a price of $227/sqft and $36.28/hr pay, less affordable than most metros. This makes it harder to attract and keep workers, as the cost of living is high.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce grew 0.4% over the past year, faster than most metros. This means employers have a growing pool of potential workers to hire from.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals fell 4.2% over the past year. This signals a potential future housing supply shortage, which could drive up prices.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 37 days to sell, up 15.6% from last year. This is a healthy housing market, giving buyers time to find a home and sellers a chance to sell.

**Office Economy**
The city's office-based workforce is about average compared to other metros. This suits businesses that need a mix of office and other skills, but may not be ideal for those requiring a deep pool of specialized talent.

The city's biggest strength is its relatively low unemployment rate, making it an attractive place for workers. However, its biggest risk is its slow job growth, which may make it harder for businesses to find the workers they need.
