# Jacksonville

**Grade: C- (Critical) | 30.7th percentile | November 2026**

---

Jacksonville has a C- overall grade, with 30.7 out of 100. The city's economy is defined by slow job growth, at 0.3% over the past year. This slow growth is a major concern.

**Labor Demand**
Job growth is 0.3% over the past year, weaker than most metros. This means people looking for work may struggle to find jobs.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 4.5%, higher than most metros. This makes it easier for businesses to hire, but also means local consumer demand may be weaker.

**Wage Growth**
Pay growth is 1.0% over the past year, slower than most metros. This means employers' labor costs are not rising quickly, but workers' paychecks are not growing much either.

**Cost of Living**
It takes 5.8 hours of pay to buy one square foot of home, at $212/sqft and $36.45/hr pay, less affordable than most metros. This makes it harder for businesses to attract workers, as the cost of living is relatively high.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce grew -0.1% over the past year, about average for the 50 metros. This means employers may not see a big increase in the labor pool, making hiring slightly more challenging.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals fell 10.8% over the past year, weaker than most metros. This signals a potential future housing supply squeeze, which could drive up prices.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 66 days to sell, down 13.2% from a year ago, a healthier housing market than most metros. This means people moving to the city can find homes relatively quickly, a plus for businesses recruiting talent.

**Office Economy**
The city's office-based workforce is about average for the 50 metros. This suits businesses that need a mix of professional and other skills, but may not be ideal for those requiring a deep tech or finance talent pool.

The city's biggest strength is its relatively healthy housing market, with homes selling quickly. However, its biggest risk is slow job growth, which may make it harder for businesses to attract and retain workers.
