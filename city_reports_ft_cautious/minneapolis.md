# Minneapolis-St. Paul-Bloomington

**Grade: C- (Critical) | 37.1th percentile | November 2026**

---

Minneapolis-St. Paul-Bloomington earns a C- grade, with an overall score of 37.1. The city's economy is defined by slower job growth, with employment rising 1.1% over the past year. This pace is near the middle compared to other metros.

**Labor Demand**
The city added 1.1% more jobs over the past year, with weekly hours 0.8% below average. This means employers are not seeing strong demand for labor, making it easier to hire.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 4.5%, higher than most metros. This makes it easier for businesses to find workers, but also signals weaker local consumer demand.

**Wage Growth**
Hourly earnings rose 0.7% over the past year, slower than most metros. This means employers face lower labor cost pressure, but workers have less bargaining power.

**Cost of Living**
It takes 5.0 hours of pay to buy one square foot of home, with prices at $204/sqft and pay at $40.79/hr. This affordability is better than most metros, making it easier to attract and keep workers.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce shrank 0.7% over the past year, slower than most metros. This means employers face a shrinking labor pool, making hiring more challenging.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals rose 10.9% over the past year, stronger than most metros. This signals growing developer confidence and a future increase in housing supply.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 43 days to sell, up 4.9% from last year, indicating a healthier market. This means buyers have more time to find a home, and the market is not too tight.

**Office Economy**
The city's office-based workforce is smaller than most metros. This suits businesses that are not heavily reliant on professional services or tech talent.

The city's biggest strength is its affordable cost of living, driven by falling home prices. However, its biggest risk is a shrinking labor force, which poses a challenge for employers trying to hire and grow their businesses.
