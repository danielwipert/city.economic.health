**Grade: C- (Critical) | 37.1th percentile | November 2026**

This slow labor force growth is a weakness for the city, as it can limit its ability to support business expansion and economic growth.

**Office Economy**
The city's office-based workforce is smaller than most other metros, with a score of 38 out of 100, which means it may not be the best fit for businesses that rely heavily on tech, finance, or professional services.
