# Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler

**Grade: C+ (Fair) | 45.8th percentile | November 2026**

---

The city earns a C+ grade. Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler's economy is defined by strong hiring, with 1.0% job growth over the past year.

**Labor Demand**
The city added jobs over the past year. Workers' hours rose 0.8%, indicating strong demand.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 4.2%. This makes it easier for employers to hire.

**Wage Growth**
Pay rose 3.2% over the past year. This means slower pay growth for workers.

**Cost of Living**
Housing is affordable, with $264/sqft and $37.74/hr pay. Prices fell 2.2% over the past year.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce shrank 3.0% over the past year. This makes hiring harder.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals fell 7.5% over the past year. This signals weaker future housing supply.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 59 days to sell, unchanged from last year. This is a weak housing market.

**Office Economy**
The city has a large office-based workforce. This suits tech, finance, and professional services businesses.

The city's biggest strength is its strong office workforce. Its biggest risk is a shrinking labor pool.
