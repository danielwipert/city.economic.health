**Grade: B- (Below Average) | 44.5th percentile | July 2026**

The Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler metro area has an overall grade of B- with a composite score ranking it at the 44.5th percentile among 50 US metros. The employment growth rate of +0.96% and weekly hours deviation of +0.193% indicate a strong labor market with genuine demand expansion. This combination signals that the city is adding jobs and workers are putting in more hours, suggesting a healthy and growing economy. The labor demand composite score of 5.84, ranking at the 82nd percentile, further reinforces this assessment.

The unemployment rate of 4.10% is slightly above the national average, ranking at the 30th percentile, indicating a moderate level of slack in the labor market. **Days on Market**

This relatively fast market means that homes are selling quickly, which can make it challenging for relocating workers to find housing. However, the decreasing days on market may indicate a normalization of the housing market, rather than a sign of demand erosion.

**Office Economy**

The city's strong office economy makes it an attractive location for businesses that require specialized knowledge workers. However, it may be less suitable for industries that rely on industrial or logistics workers.

The Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler metro area offers businesses a strong labor demand and a relatively affordable cost of living, making it an attractive location for talent.
