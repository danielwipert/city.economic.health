# Columbus

**Grade: C+ (Fair) | 47.7th percentile | November 2026**

---

Columbus earns a C+ grade. The city's economy is defined by low unemployment, at 3.3%.

**Labor Demand**
The city lost 0.1% of its jobs over the past year. Workers' hours rose 0.5%, but job losses mean remaining workers are putting in extra hours.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 3.3%. This makes it hard for employers to hire, with low unemployment.

**Wage Growth**
Pay rose 4.5% over the past year. Faster pay growth means rising labor costs for employers.

**Cost of Living**
Housing costs $205 per square foot, with hourly pay at $34.52. This is about average for affordability.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce shrank 2.0% over the past year. This slows hiring, with a shrinking labor pool.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals fell 3.0% over the past year. This signals a neutral future housing supply.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 50 days to sell, up 6.4% from last year. This is a healthier housing market, with more time for buyers.

**Office Economy**
The office-based workforce is smaller than most metros. This suits industrial or logistics businesses better.

The city's biggest strength is its low unemployment. Its biggest risk is a shrinking workforce, making hiring harder.
