# Washington-Arlington-Alexandria

**Grade: C+ (Fair) | 49.0th percentile | November 2026**

---

The city earns a C+ grade. Its economy is defined by slow job growth, with a 1.8% decline over the past year.

**Labor Demand**
The city lost jobs over the past year. Workers' hours are also falling.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 3.7%. This makes it hard for employers to hire.

**Wage Growth**
Pay rose 6.0% over the past year. This means higher labor costs for employers.

**Cost of Living**
Housing is affordable, with $299/sqft and $45.44/hr pay. Prices are falling 2.9% from a year ago.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce is shrinking 2.2% over the past year. This makes hiring harder.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals rose 13.9% over the past year. This signals stronger future housing supply.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 43 days to sell, up 13.2% from a year ago. This is a healthy housing market.

**Office Economy**
The office workforce is smaller than most metros. This suits industrial or logistics businesses.

The city's biggest strength is its low unemployment rate. Its biggest risk is slow labor force growth.
