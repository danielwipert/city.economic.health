# Washington-Arlington-Alexandria

**Grade: C+ (Fair) | 49.0th percentile | November 2026**

---

The city of Washington-Arlington-Alexandria has a fair economy, with an overall grade of C+. This is largely due to its low unemployment rate of 3.7% and strong wage growth of 6.0% over the past year. The city's economy is defined by its tight labor market and rising pay.

**Labor Demand**
The city's job growth is -1.8% over the past year, weaker than most metros. This means people looking for work may face challenges finding a job.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 3.7%, lower than most metros. This makes hiring harder for employers due to a smaller pool of available workers.

**Wage Growth**
Pay growth is 6.0% over the past year, faster than most metros. This means employers face rising labor costs, while workers see bigger paychecks.

**Cost of Living**
It takes 6.6 hours of local pay to buy one square foot of home, at $299/sqft and $45.44/hr pay, more affordable than most metros. This attracts and keeps workers, as the cost of living is relatively low.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce grew -2.2% over the past year, slower than most metros. This makes it harder for employers to hire due to a shrinking labor pool.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals rose 13.9% over the past year, stronger than most metros. This signals a future increase in housing supply, which could improve affordability.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 43 days to sell, up 13.2% from last year, a healthier market than most metros. This means people moving to the city have a relatively easy time finding a home.

**Office Economy**
The city's office-based workforce is smaller than most metros. This suits businesses that are not heavily reliant on tech, finance, or professional services.

The city's biggest strength is its strong wage growth, making it attractive to workers. However, its biggest risk is its slow labor force growth, making it challenging for employers to find new hires.
