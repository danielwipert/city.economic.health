# Seattle-Tacoma-Bellevue

**Grade: B- (Below Average) | 51.0th percentile | November 2026**

---

The city of Seattle-Tacoma-Bellevue has a B- overall grade. It is defined by strong job growth, with a 0.7% increase in employment over the past year.

**Labor Demand**
The city added jobs over the past year. Workers' hours are also rising, indicating genuine demand.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 4.9%. This makes it easier for employers to hire.

**Wage Growth**
Pay rose 5.5% over the past year. This means higher labor costs for employers and more money in workers' paychecks.

**Cost of Living**
Housing is about average in affordability, with $423 per square foot and $47.99 hourly pay. Prices are falling 3.2% from a year ago.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce is growing 1.4% faster than most metros. This means a larger pool of workers for hiring.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals fell 13.6% over the past year. This signals a weaker future housing supply.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 51 days to sell, up 6.2% from a year ago. This is a healthier housing market.

**Office Economy**
The city has a large office-based workforce. This suits tech, finance, and professional services businesses.

The city's biggest strength is its fast labor force growth. Its biggest risk is a weakening housing market due to falling building permits.
