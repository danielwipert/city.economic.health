# Salt Lake City-Murray

**Grade: A+ (Excellent) | 77.6th percentile | November 2026**

---

Salt Lake City earns an A+ grade. The city's economy is defined by strong job growth, with a 2.7% increase in employment over the past year.

**Labor Demand**
The city is adding jobs. Workers' hours are slightly below average.

**Unemployment**
Unemployment is 3.2%. Hiring is harder due to low unemployment.

**Wage Growth**
Pay rose 4.9% over the past year. Faster wage growth means higher labor costs.

**Cost of Living**
Housing is affordable at $260/sqft. Prices rose slightly over the past year.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce grew 1.2% over the past year. A growing labor pool helps hiring.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals rose 25.9% over the past year. Stronger home-building signals more housing supply.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 63 days to sell, up 6.8% from last year. The housing market is softer.

**Office Economy**
The city has a large office-based workforce. This suits tech, finance, and professional services.

The city's biggest strength is its strong job market. Its biggest risk is a softening housing market.
