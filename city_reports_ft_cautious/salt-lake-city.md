**Office Economy**
Salt Lake City-Murray boasts a deep professional talent pool, ranking in the top tier at the 96th percentile. However, the tight labor market and rising wages may pose challenges for companies looking to hire and expand in this location, making it essential for decision-makers to factor in these constraints when considering Salt Lake City-Murray as a potential business location.
