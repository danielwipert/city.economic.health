# Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach

**Grade: A- (Good) | 65.2th percentile | November 2026**

---

Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach earns an A- grade. The city's economy is defined by strong wage growth, with pay rising 8.8% over the past year.

**Labor Demand**
The city added 0.4% more jobs over the past year. Workers' hours are steady, indicating a slow job market.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 3.2%. This makes it hard for employers to hire, with low unemployment.

**Wage Growth**
Pay rose 8.8% over the past year. This means higher labor costs for employers and bigger paychecks for workers.

**Cost of Living**
Housing costs $358 per square foot, down 0.6% from a year ago, versus $35.70 hourly pay. The city is less affordable than most metros.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce grew 0.9% over the past year. This means a growing pool of workers for employers.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals rose 11.3% over the past year. This signals a stronger future housing supply.

**Days on Market**
Homes sell in 79 days, down 8.1% from a year ago. The housing market is healthy, with homes selling faster.

**Office Economy**
The city has a large office-based workforce. This suits tech, finance, and professional services businesses.

The city's biggest strength is its strong wage growth. Its biggest risk is a less affordable cost of living, making it harder to attract workers.
