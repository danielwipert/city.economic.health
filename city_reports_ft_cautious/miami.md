# Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach

**Grade: A- (Good) | 65.2th percentile | November 2026**

---

Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach earns an overall grade of A-, with a score of 65.2. The city's economy is defined by strong wage growth, with hourly earnings rising 8.8% over the past year. This rapid pay growth is a key characteristic of the city's labor market.

**Labor Demand**
Job growth is 0.4% over the past year, weaker than most metros. This slower hiring pace makes it easier for employers to find workers.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 3.2%, lower than most metros. This tight labor market makes hiring more challenging for employers.

**Wage Growth**
Pay growth is 8.8% over the past year, faster than most metros. This rapid wage growth increases labor costs for employers but boosts workers' paychecks.

**Cost of Living**
It takes 10.0 hours of pay to buy one square foot of home, with a price of $358 per square foot and hourly pay of $35.70, making it less affordable than most metros. This higher cost of living may deter some workers from moving to the city.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce is growing 0.9% over the past year, faster than most metros. This expanding labor pool gives employers a larger talent pool to hire from.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals are up 11.3% over the past year. This increase in building permits signals a growing housing supply and potentially more affordable prices.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 79 days to sell, with sales happening 8.1% faster than last year. This healthier housing market makes it easier for people to buy and sell homes.

**Office Economy**
The city has a larger office-based workforce than most metros. This suits businesses in tech, finance, and professional services, which require a deep talent pool.

The city's biggest strength is its rapid wage growth, which attracts workers and boosts consumer spending. However, its high cost of living is a significant risk, as it may deter some workers from moving to the city and increase labor costs for employers.
