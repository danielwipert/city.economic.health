# Louisville-Jefferson County

**Grade: C- (Critical) | 27.0th percentile | September 2026**

The Louisville-Jefferson County metro area has an overall grade of C- with a composite score ranking it at the 27.0th percentile out of 50 US metros. This suggests that remaining workers are absorbing the load of eliminated roles.

This means that businesses may find it relatively easier to hire workers, but may also face weaker local consumer demand. **Wage Growth**

The slow wage growth may limit the attractiveness of the city to businesses looking for a highly skilled and highly paid workforce.

This indicates a slower market, making it more accessible for workers relocating to the city.
