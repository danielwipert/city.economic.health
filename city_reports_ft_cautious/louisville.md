# Louisville-Jefferson County

**Grade: D (Emergency) | 18.7th percentile | August 2026**

The Louisville-Jefferson County metro area has an overall grade of D, ranking 18.7th percentile out of 50 US metros, with a labor demand composite score of 2.57. **Unemployment**
