# Hartford-West Hartford-East Hartford

**Grade: A+ (Excellent) | 73.0th percentile | November 2026**

---

Hartford earns an A+ grade. The city's economy is defined by strong job growth, with a 1.9% increase in employment over the past year.

**Labor Demand**
The city added jobs over the past year. Workers' hours are rising, indicating a strong job market.

**Unemployment**
Unemployment is 2.7%. This makes it hard for employers to hire.

**Wage Growth**
Pay rose 5.0% over the past year. This means higher labor costs for employers.

**Cost of Living**
Housing costs $246 per square foot. This is about average for affordability.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce grew 2.8% over the past year. This helps employers hire.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals fell 53.9% over the past year. This signals a future housing shortage.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 37 days to sell, up 2.8% from last year. This is a relatively healthy market.

**Office Economy**
The city has a small office-based workforce. This suits industrial or logistics businesses.

The city's biggest strength is its strong job market. Its biggest risk is a potential housing shortage.
