# Sacramento-Roseville-Folsom

**Grade: C (Very Poor) | 38.6th percentile | November 2026**

---

The Sacramento-Roseville-Folsom metro area has an overall grade of C, indicating a very poor economic performance. The city's economy is defined by slow wage growth, with hourly earnings rising only 2.6% over the past year. This slow growth is a major concern for workers and employers alike.

**Labor Demand**
The city's job growth is 0.8% over the past year, better than most metros. This means people looking for work have a relatively good chance of finding a job.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 5.0%, higher than most metros. This makes it easier for employers to find staff, but also indicates weaker local consumer demand.

**Wage Growth**
Pay growth is 2.6% over the past year, slower than most metros. This means employers have lower labor costs, but workers have less bargaining power.

**Cost of Living**
It takes 8.9 hours of local pay to buy one square foot of home, at $338/sqft and $38.15/hr pay, making it less affordable than most metros. This makes it harder to attract and keep workers, and affordability is not improving.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce is shrinking 1.3% over the past year, slower than most metros. This makes it harder for employers to find new hires.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals are up 15.8% over the past year, stronger than most metros. This signals a future increase in housing supply, which could improve affordability.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 52 days to sell, down 10.3% from last year, but still a weaker housing market. This means buyers have less time to find a home, making it harder for people to move here.

**Office Economy**
The city's office-based workforce is smaller than most metros. This makes it less suitable for tech, finance, and professional services businesses.

The city's biggest strength is its strong job growth, with hiring outpacing most metros. However, its biggest risk is its slow wage growth, which may lead to a brain drain as workers seek better-paying opportunities elsewhere.
