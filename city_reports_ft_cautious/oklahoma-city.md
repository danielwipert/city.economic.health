The city's unemployment rate of 4.20% is below average, ranking it in the 24th percentile. The current median days on market in Oklahoma City is 52 days, with a year-over-year increase of 6.1%, ranking it in the 80th percentile. **Office Economy**
Oklahoma City's professional and office worker share ranks in the 6th percentile, indicating a relatively shallow talent pool in these sectors. This suggests that the city is better suited for businesses that do not require a deep knowledge-economy talent pool, such as industrial or logistics companies, rather than tech, finance, or consulting firms.

The single biggest risk or constraint for decision-makers is the city's limited labor force growth and tightening housing supply, which may lead to increased competition for talent and decreased affordability in the future.
