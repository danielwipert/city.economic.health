# St. Louis

**Grade: A+ (Excellent) | 73.7th percentile | November 2026**

---

St. Louis earns an A+ grade. The city's economy is defined by strong hiring and affordable housing, with 1.0% job growth over the past year.

**Labor Demand**
The city added 1.0% more jobs. Workers' hours rose 1.0%, showing genuine demand.

**Unemployment**
Unemployment is 3.4%. This makes it hard for employers to hire.

**Wage Growth**
Pay rose 4.3% over the past year. This means higher labor costs and bigger paychecks.

**Cost of Living**
Housing is affordable at $163/sqft. Prices fell 1.8% over the past year.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce shrank 0.1% over the past year. This makes hiring slightly harder.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals rose 7.4% over the past year. This signals more future housing supply.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 51 days to sell, up 13.3% from last year. This is a healthier housing market.

**Office Economy**
The office workforce is smaller than most metros. This suits industrial or logistics businesses.

The city's biggest strength is its affordable housing. Its biggest risk is a smaller office workforce.
