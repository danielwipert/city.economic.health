# Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands

**Grade: C+ (Fair) | 45.1th percentile | November 2026**

---

Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands has a C+ overall grade, with a score of 45.1 out of 100. The city's economy is defined by its slow employment growth, with a 1.0% increase over the past year. This growth is near the middle compared to other metros.

**Building Permits**

**Days on Market**
Homes take 57 days to sell, about the same as a year ago. This indicates a weaker housing market, making it harder for people to move here.

**Office Economy**
The city has a large office-based workforce, better than most metros. This suits businesses in tech, finance, and professional services, which require a deep talent pool.

The city's biggest strength is its affordable cost of living, which helps attract workers. Its biggest risk is a weak housing market, which could make it harder for people to relocate here.
