# Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands

**Grade: C+ (Fair) | 45.1th percentile | November 2026**

---

Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands has a C+ overall grade, with a score of 45.1 out of 100. The city's economy is defined by its slow employment growth, with a 1.0% increase over the past year. This growth is near the middle compared to other metros.

**Labor Demand**
The city added 1.0% more jobs over the past year, with weekly hours 0.5% below average. This means employers are hiring, but workers are putting in fewer hours.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 4.6%, higher than most metros. This makes it easier for employers to find staff, but weaker local consumer demand.

**Wage Growth**
Pay grew 2.2% over the past year, slower than most metros. This means lower labor costs for employers, but weaker bargaining power for workers.

**Cost of Living**

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce grew 0.7% over the past year, faster than most metros. This means employers have a growing pool of potential hires.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals fell 9.2% over the past year. This signals a potential future housing supply squeeze and higher prices.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 57 days to sell, about the same as last year. This indicates a weaker housing market, making it harder for people to move here.

**Office Economy**
The city has a large office-based workforce, better than most metros. This suits businesses in tech, finance, and professional services.

The city's biggest strength is its affordable cost of living, driven by falling home prices. Its biggest risk is a weakening housing market, which could make it harder to attract workers and drive up prices.
