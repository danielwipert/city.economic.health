# Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands

**Grade: C+ (Fair) | 45.1th percentile | November 2026**

---

Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands has a C+ overall grade. The city's economy is defined by slow job growth, with a 1.0% increase over the past year.

**Labor Demand**
The city added jobs, but at a slow pace. Workers' hours are slightly below average.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 4.6%. This makes it easier for employers to hire.

**Wage Growth**
Pay rose 2.2% over the past year. This is slower than most metros.

**Cost of Living**
Housing is affordable, with $168/sqft and $37.07/hr pay. Prices are falling, making it even more affordable.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce is growing at 0.7% per year. This is faster than most metros.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals fell 9.2% over the past year. This signals a weaker housing supply ahead.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 57 days to sell, unchanged from last year. This is a weak housing market.

**Office Economy**
The city has a large office-based workforce. This suits tech, finance, and professional services businesses.

The city's biggest strength is its affordable housing. Its biggest risk is a slow job market with high unemployment.
