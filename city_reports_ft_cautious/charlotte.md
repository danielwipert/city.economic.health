**Grade: A+ (Excellent) | 73.3th percentile | July 2026**

The Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia metro area earns an overall grade of A+ with a composite score of 73.3th percentile, ranking it among the top US metros. The combination of a 1.37% employment growth rate and a 0.945% increase in weekly hours signals genuine demand expansion in the Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia metro area. This indicates that the city is experiencing a surge in job creation, with hours worked above the trend, suggesting a strong and growing economy. This rate indicates a relatively tight labor market, although not extremely so, suggesting that businesses may face some competition for talent but not to an extreme degree. **Wage Growth**

This growth in permits is a positive indicator for future affordability and the ability to accommodate a growing workforce, suggesting that the city is likely to remain attractive to workers and businesses alike in terms of housing availability and costs.

However, the market remains competitive, and workers may still face challenges in finding and securing housing quickly.

The Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia metro area offers businesses a strong economy with genuine labor demand expansion, a moderate unemployment rate, and a relatively affordable cost of living.
