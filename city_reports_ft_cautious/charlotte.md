# Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia

**Grade: A+ (Excellent) | 72.4th percentile | November 2026**

---

The city earns an overall grade of A+, with a score of 72.4 out of 100. This is driven by strong labor demand, with employment growth of 1.8% over the past year. The city's unemployment rate is also low, at 3.3%.

**Labor Demand**
The city added 1.8% more jobs over the past year, with weekly hours rising 1.0%. This means people looking for work have a good chance of finding a job with decent hours.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 3.3%, lower than most metros. This makes it harder for employers to hire, as there are fewer people looking for work.

**Wage Growth**
Pay grew 4.4% over the past year, faster than most metros. This means employers face rising labor costs, while workers see bigger paychecks.

**Cost of Living**
It takes 5.6 hours of pay to buy one square foot of home, with prices at $216 per square foot and pay at $38.60 per hour. This is more affordable than most metros, making it easier to attract and keep workers.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce grew 0.7% over the past year, faster than most metros. This means employers have a growing pool of potential hires to choose from.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals fell 12.7% over the past year. This signals a potential future housing shortage, which could drive up prices.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 65 days to sell, up 10.2% from a year ago. This is a softening housing market, making it harder for people to move here.

**Office Economy**
The city has a large office-based workforce, bigger than most metros. This suits businesses in tech, finance, and professional services, which thrive in this environment.

The city's biggest strength is its strong labor demand, driven by job growth and rising hours. However, its biggest risk is a softening housing market, which could make it harder for people to move here and for employers to attract talent.
