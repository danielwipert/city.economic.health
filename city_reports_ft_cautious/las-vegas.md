# Las Vegas-Henderson-North Las Vegas

**Grade: B (Average) | 59.8th percentile | November 2026**

---

Las Vegas earns a B grade, with 1.7% job growth over the past year. The city's economy is defined by strong hiring and rising worker hours.

**Labor Demand**
The city added 1.7% more jobs over the past year. Workers' hours rose 1.1%, indicating a strong job market.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 4.8%. This makes it about average for employers to hire.

**Wage Growth**
Pay rose 3.6% over the past year. This means moderate cost increases for employers and decent pay raises for workers.

**Cost of Living**
Housing costs $258 per square foot, down 2.6% from last year, with $32.90 hourly pay. This makes housing about average in affordability.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce shrank 0.6% over the past year. This slows hiring and creates a challenge.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals fell 4.9% over the past year. This signals a potential future housing supply shortage.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 59 days to sell, up 1.7% from last year. This indicates a slightly softening housing market.

**Office Economy**
The city has a large office-based workforce. This suits tech, finance, and professional services businesses.

The city's biggest strength is its strong job market with rising hours. Its biggest risk is a shrinking workforce, making hiring more challenging.
