# Virginia Beach-Chesapeake-Norfolk

**Grade: C- (Critical) | 35.6th percentile | November 2026**

---

The city earns a C- grade, with a weak job market defined by -0.2% employment growth over the past year. This slow hiring pace is a major concern, with workers' hours also falling 0.1% from their 12-month average.

**Labor Demand**
The city lost jobs over the past year, with employment down 0.2%. This decline means a weaker job market.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 3.8%, making it about average for hiring. Employers face moderate difficulty finding workers.

**Wage Growth**
Pay rose 4.5% over the past year, outpacing most metros. Faster wage growth means higher labor costs for employers.

**Cost of Living**
Housing costs $222 per square foot, up 2.3% from last year, versus $34.92 hourly pay. The city is less affordable than most metros.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce shrank 1.4% over the past year, slowing hiring. A declining labor pool makes it harder to find workers.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals fell 17.5% over the past year, signaling a weaker housing supply. This decline may worsen affordability.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 45 days to sell, up 2.3% from last year, indicating a softening market. The housing market is about average.

**Office Economy**
The city's office workforce is smaller than most metros, with a limited talent pool. This suits industrial or logistics businesses better.

The city's biggest strength is its fast wage growth, benefiting workers. However, its biggest risk is a weak job market, with slow hiring and a shrinking workforce.
