**Grade: C (Very Poor) | 41.2th percentile | October 2026**

The Virginia Beach-Chesapeake-Norfolk metro area presents a mixed bag for businesses considering relocation or expansion, with fast wage growth and a relatively tight labor market being positives, but labor demand contraction, shrinking labor force, and tightening housing supply being significant negatives.
