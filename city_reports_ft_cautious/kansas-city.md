# Kansas City

**Grade: B+ (Above Average) | 62.8th percentile | November 2026**

---

Kansas City earns a B+ grade. The city's economy is defined by strong wage growth, with pay rising 7.6% over the past year.

**Labor Demand**
The city added 0.5% more jobs over the past year. Workers' hours rose 0.2%, indicating a stable job market.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 3.4%. This makes it harder for employers to hire, with low unemployment.

**Wage Growth**
Pay rose 7.6% over the past year. This means higher labor costs for employers, but more money in workers' paychecks.

**Cost of Living**
Housing is affordable, with $194 per square foot and $36.10 hourly pay. Affordability is improving, despite prices rising 1.6% over the past year.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce shrank 2.1% over the past year. This makes hiring harder, with a smaller labor pool.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals rose 96.0% over the past year. This signals a stronger future housing supply.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 52 days to sell, up 2.0% from last year. The housing market is average, with homes selling neither too fast nor too slow.

**Office Economy**
The city has a smaller office-based workforce. This suits industrial or logistics businesses better than tech or finance.

The city's biggest strength is its strong wage growth. Its biggest risk is a shrinking workforce, making hiring harder.
