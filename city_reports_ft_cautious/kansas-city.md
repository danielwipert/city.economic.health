# Kansas City

**Grade: B+ (Above Average) | 62.8th percentile | November 2026**

---

Kansas City earns a B+ grade, with an overall score of 62.8. The city's economy is defined by strong wage growth, with a 7.6% increase over the past year. This growth is a key factor in the city's attractiveness to businesses and workers.

**Labor Demand**
The city's job growth is 0.5% over the past year, about average for most metros. This means employers may face some challenges finding new talent, but it's not extremely difficult.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 3.4%, lower than most metros. This makes hiring more competitive, as there are fewer available workers.

**Wage Growth**
Pay growth is 7.6% over the past year, faster than most metros. This means employers face rising labor costs, but workers see significant increases in their paychecks.

**Cost of Living**
It takes 5.4 hours of local pay to buy one square foot of home, priced at $194/sqft, making it more affordable than most metros. This attracts and retains workers, as the cost of living is relatively low.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce has shrunk 2.1% over the past year, slower than most metros. This makes it harder for employers to find new talent, as the labor pool is decreasing.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals have increased 96.0% over the past year, stronger than most metros. This signals a growing housing supply, which should improve affordability and attract more workers.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 52 days to sell, up 2.0% from last year, about average for most metros. This indicates a relatively balanced housing market, neither too tight nor too soft.

**Office Economy**
The city's office-based workforce is smaller than most metros. This makes it less suitable for businesses that rely on a large pool of tech, finance, or professional services talent.

The city's biggest strength is its strong wage growth, making it an attractive location for workers. However, its biggest risk is the shrinking labor force, which may make it harder for employers to find the talent they need.
