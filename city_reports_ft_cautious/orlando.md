# Orlando-Kissimmee-Sanford

**Grade: B+ (Above Average) | 61.0th percentile | November 2026**

---

Orlando-Kissimmee-Sanford earns a B+ grade. The city's economy is defined by strong hiring, with 1.0% job growth over the past year. Weekly hours are also up 0.6%.

**Labor Demand**
The city sees 1.0% job growth and 0.6% more weekly hours, better than most metros. This means people looking for work have a good chance of finding a job.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 4.2%, higher than most metros. This makes it easier for businesses to hire, but also signals weaker local consumer demand.

**Wage Growth**
Pay is growing 6.0% over the past year, faster than most metros. This means employers face rising labor costs, while workers see bigger paychecks.

**Cost of Living**
It takes 6.6 hours of pay to buy one square foot of home, with prices at $222/sqft and pay at $33.42/hr, more affordable than most metros. This attracts workers, and affordability is improving as prices fall 2.6% over the past year.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce is growing 0.7% over the past year, faster than most metros. This helps employers find new hires, as the labor pool expands.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals are down 12.2% over the past year. This signals a potential future housing supply squeeze, putting upward pressure on prices.

**Days on Market**
Homes sell in 78 days, down 3.7% from last year. This is a healthy housing market, with homes selling relatively quickly, making it easier for people to move here.

**Office Economy**
The city has a large office-based workforce, better than most metros. This suits businesses in tech, finance, and professional services, which thrive in this environment.

The city's biggest strength is its strong wage growth, making it attractive to workers. Its biggest risk is the decline in building permits, which may lead to a housing supply shortage and higher prices.
