**Grade: C- (Critical) | 33.6th percentile | November 2026**

---

The Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario metro area has an overall grade of C-, with a score of 33.6 out of 100. This city's economy is defined by its slow job growth, with only 0.6% more jobs added over the past year. The unemployment rate is also a concern, at 5.7%.

**Wage Growth**

**Building Permits**

**Days on Market**
Homes take 62 days to sell, down 1.6% from last year, about average for most metros. This is a relatively healthy housing market, but buyers have limited time to find a home.

**Office Economy**
The office-based workforce is smaller than most metros. This suits businesses with more industrial or logistics-focused operations.

The city's biggest strength is its fast wage growth, giving workers more purchasing power. However, its biggest risk is its high cost of living, making it hard to attract and retain workers due to expensive housing.
