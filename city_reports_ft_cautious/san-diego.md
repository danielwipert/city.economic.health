# San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad

**Grade: B- (Below Average) | 50.3th percentile | November 2026**

---

San Diego earns a B- grade. The city's economy is defined by strong job growth, with a 1.3% increase in employment over the past year.

**Labor Demand**
The city is adding jobs. Workers' hours are rising, indicating a strong job market.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 4.7%. This makes it harder for employers to hire.

**Wage Growth**
Pay rose 7.8% over the past year. This means higher labor costs for employers.

**Cost of Living**
Housing is less affordable, with $583 per square foot. Prices are falling, but slowly.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce is shrinking, down 0.8% over the past year. This makes hiring harder.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals fell 16.7% over the past year. This signals a future housing shortage.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 48 days to sell, up 2.1% from last year. The market is softening.

**Office Economy**
The city has a large office workforce. This suits tech and finance businesses.

The city's biggest strength is its strong job growth. Its biggest risk is a shrinking workforce, making hiring harder.
