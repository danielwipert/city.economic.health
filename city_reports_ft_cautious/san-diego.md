# San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad

**Grade: B- (Below Average) | 50.3th percentile | November 2026**

---

San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad has a B- overall grade, with a score of 50.3 out of 100. The city's economy is defined by strong job growth, with a 1.3% increase over the past year. This growth is accompanied by a 7.8% rise in hourly earnings.

**Labor Demand**
The city has 1.3% job growth and 0.5% higher weekly hours, better than most metros. This means people looking for work have a good chance of finding a job with decent hours.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 4.7%, higher than most metros. This makes it easier for businesses to find staff, but also indicates weaker local consumer demand.

**Wage Growth**
Hourly earnings grew 7.8% over the past year, faster than most metros. This means employers face rising labor costs, while workers enjoy higher paychecks.

**Cost of Living**
It takes 13.4 hours of pay to buy one square foot of home, with a price of $583/sqft, down 3.6% from last year, but still less affordable than most metros. This makes it harder to attract and keep workers, despite some improvement.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce shrank 0.8% over the past year, slower than most metros. This makes it harder for employers to find new hires.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals fell 16.7% over the past year. This signals a future housing supply squeeze, which may drive up prices.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 48 days to sell, up 2.1% from last year. This indicates a slightly softening housing market, giving buyers a bit more time to find a home.

**Office Economy**
The city has a larger office-based workforce than most metros. This suits businesses in tech, finance, and professional services.

The city's biggest strength is its strong wage growth, driven by a 7.8% rise in hourly earnings, making it attractive for workers. However, its biggest risk is a less affordable cost of living, with homes priced at $583/sqft, making it harder to attract and retain talent.
