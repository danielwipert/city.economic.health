# San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad

**Grade: C (Very Poor) | 37.5th percentile | September 2026**

The San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad metro area has an overall grade of C, ranking 37.5th percentile out of 50 US metros, with a composite score defined largely by its low labor demand and high wage growth. This decline in labor force growth signals a structural headwind for hiring, as the supply of potential workers is decreasing. Businesses may face challenges in finding and recruiting talent in this environment.

This city is well-suited for businesses requiring specialized knowledge-economy roles but may be less ideal for those with primarily industrial or logistics-dominant operations.

The San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad metro area offers businesses a unique combination of high wage growth and a deep professional talent pool, but the single biggest risk or constraint is the city's relatively high cost of living and shrinking labor force, which could hinder talent attraction and hiring capacity. Decision-makers should carefully weigh these factors when considering this location for their business operations. Overall, the city's economic character is complex, with both opportunities and challenges that need to be carefully navigated.
