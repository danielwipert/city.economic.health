**Grade: A- (Good) | 62.2th percentile | August 2026**

The Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro area has an overall grade of A- with a composite score ranking at the 62.2th percentile among 50 US metros. The employment growth rate of 1.89% and the -0.596% deviation in weekly hours from its own trend signal a genuine demand expansion, as hours are not excessively above trend during job growth. **Office Economy**
With an office/professional worker share composite score of 4.22, ranking at the 94th percentile, Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos has a deep and specialized talent pool. This makes the city well-suited for businesses in the tech, finance, consulting, and HQ sectors, but less ideal for those in industrial or logistics-dominant economies.
