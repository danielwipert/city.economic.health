# Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos

**Grade: A+ (Excellent) | 73.6th percentile | November 2026**

---

The city of Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos earns an overall grade of A+, with a strong economy driven by job growth of 1.1% over the past year. This growth is accompanied by a rise in weekly hours worked, indicating genuine demand for labor. The city's cost of living is also a notable strength, with homes becoming more affordable.

**Labor Demand**
The city's job growth is 1.1% over the past year, better than most metros. This means people looking for work have a good chance of finding a job.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 3.7%, about average for the 50 metros. This makes hiring neither very easy nor very hard for employers.

**Wage Growth**
Pay is growing 5.4% over the past year, faster than most metros. This means employers face rising labor costs, while workers see bigger paychecks.

**Cost of Living**
It takes 6.0 hours of pay to buy one square foot of home, at $226/sqft (down 8.5% from a year ago) and $37.91/hr pay, making it more affordable than most metros. This attracts and keeps workers, as the cost of living is getting better.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce is growing 1.0% over the past year, faster than most metros. This helps employers find the talent they need to hire.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals are up 15.3% over the past year. This signals a growing housing supply, which should improve affordability and prices.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 84 days to sell, up 6.3% from a year ago, making the housing market weaker. This means people moving to the city may find more homes available, but buyers are also pulling back.

**Office Economy**
The city has a large office-based workforce, better than most metros. This suits businesses in tech, finance, and professional services, which thrive in this environment.

The city's biggest strength is its affordable cost of living, making it attractive to workers and businesses alike. However, the biggest risk is the softening housing market, which may make it harder for people to buy or sell homes, affecting the local economy.
