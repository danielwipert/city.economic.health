# Atlanta-Sandy Springs-Roswell

**Grade: C (Very Poor) | 42.9th percentile | November 2026**

---

Atlanta-Sandy Springs-Roswell has a C overall grade, with a score of 42.9 out of 100. The city's economy is defined by slower job growth, with a -0.1% change over the past year. This weakness is a key challenge for the city.

**Labor Demand**
Job growth is -0.1% over the past year, weaker than most metros. This means fewer new jobs are being added, making it harder for people to find work.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 3.5%, lower than most metros. This makes hiring harder for employers, as there are fewer available workers.

**Wage Growth**
Pay growth is +2.9% over the past year, slower than most metros. This means employers have lower labor costs, but workers have less bargaining power.

**Cost of Living**
It takes 5.2 hours of pay to buy one square foot of home, with a price of $193/sqft and hourly pay of $36.77. This affordability is about average, making it neither a major advantage nor disadvantage for attracting workers.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce is growing +2.6% over the past year, faster than most metros. This means employers have a growing pool of potential workers to hire from.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals are up +1.4% over the past year. This signals a modest increase in future housing supply, which could help keep prices in check.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 61 days to sell, up 1.7% from a year ago. This slower pace means the housing market is softer, making it easier for buyers to find homes, but potentially harder for sellers.

**Office Economy**
The city's office-based workforce is smaller than most metros. This makes it less suitable for businesses that rely on a deep pool of tech, finance, or professional services talent.

The city's biggest strength is its fast-growing workforce, which provides a large pool of potential workers for employers. However, its biggest risk is its slow job growth, which can make it harder for people to find work and for employers to expand their operations.
