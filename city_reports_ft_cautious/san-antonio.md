# San Antonio-New Braunfels

**Grade: C+ (Fair) | 49.7th percentile | November 2026**

---

San Antonio-New Braunfels has a C+ overall grade, with 49.7 points out of 100. The city's economy is defined by its slow job growth, with only 0.4% more jobs added over the past year. This slow growth is a key challenge for the local economy.

**Labor Demand**
The city added 0.4% more jobs over the past year, weaker than most metros. This means people looking for work may face a tough job market.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 4.3%, higher than most metros. This makes it easier for businesses to find staff, but also indicates weaker local consumer demand.

**Wage Growth**
Pay grew 3.9% over the past year, about average for the 50 metros. This means employers face moderate labor cost increases, while workers see decent pay raises.

**Cost of Living**
It takes 5.2 hours of local pay to buy one square foot of home, at $169/sqft (down 3.4% from a year ago) and $32.51/hr pay, making it more affordable than most metros. This attracts and retains workers, as the cost of living is getting better.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce grew 0.2% over the past year, about average for the 50 metros. This means employers have a moderately expanding pool of potential hires.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals rose 24.4% over the past year, stronger than most metros. This signals a growing housing supply and potentially improving affordability.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 74 days to sell, about the same as a year ago, a weaker housing market than most metros. This indicates a softening market, making it harder for people to move here.

**Office Economy**
The city has a large office-based workforce, better than most metros. This suits businesses in tech, finance, and professional services, which thrive in such an environment.

The city's biggest strength is its affordable cost of living, making it attractive to workers. However, its biggest risk is its slow job growth, which may limit opportunities for residents and businesses.
