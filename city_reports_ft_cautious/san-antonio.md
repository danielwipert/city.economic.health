# San Antonio-New Braunfels

**Grade: C+ (Fair) | 49.7th percentile | November 2026**

---

San Antonio-New Braunfels has a C+ overall grade, with 49.7 out of 100. The city's economy is defined by slower job growth, at 0.4% over the past year. Housing is relatively affordable, with homes costing $169 per square foot.

**Labor Demand**
Job growth is 0.4% over the past year, weaker than most metros. This means fewer new jobs are being added, making it harder for people to find work.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 4.3%, higher than most metros. This makes it easier for employers to find staff, but also means weaker local consumer demand.

**Wage Growth**
Pay is growing 3.9% over the past year, about average for most metros. This means employers face moderate labor cost increases, while workers see decent pay raises.

**Cost of Living**
Homes cost $169 per square foot, or 5.2 hours of local pay, more affordable than most metros. This attracts workers, as the cost of living is relatively low and falling, with home prices down 3.4% from a year ago.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce is growing 0.2% over the past year, about average for most metros. This means employers have a moderately expanding pool of potential workers to hire from.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals are up 24.4% over the past year, stronger than most metros. This signals a future increase in housing supply, which should improve affordability and help attract workers.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 74 days to sell, about the same as a year ago, weaker than most metros. This indicates a softening housing market, making it harder for people to buy or sell homes quickly.

**Office Economy**
The city has a large office-based workforce, better than most metros. This suits businesses in tech, finance, and professional services, which can tap into a deep talent pool.

The city's biggest strength is its affordable housing, with homes costing $169 per square foot. However, its biggest risk is a slow job market, with only 0.4% growth over the past year, making it harder for people to find work.
