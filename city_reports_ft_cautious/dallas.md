# Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington

**Grade: B+ (Above Average) | 63.3th percentile | November 2026**

---

The city earns a B+ grade. It's defined by strong job growth, with a 1.0% increase in employment over the past year.

**Labor Demand**
The city added jobs over the past year. Workers' hours are rising, indicating genuine demand.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 4.1%. This makes hiring somewhat challenging for employers.

**Wage Growth**
Pay rose 4.7% over the past year. This means higher labor costs for employers and fatter paychecks for workers.

**Cost of Living**
Housing is affordable, with $197 per square foot and $37.92 hourly pay. Prices are falling, making it even more affordable.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce grew 0.6% over the past year. This means a slightly expanding pool of workers for hiring.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals fell 8.4% over the past year. This signals a potential future housing supply squeeze.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 64 days to sell, unchanged from last year. This indicates a relatively soft housing market.

**Office Economy**
The city has a large office-based workforce. This suits tech, finance, and professional services businesses.

The city's biggest strength is its strong job market. Its biggest risk is a potential housing supply squeeze, which could drive up costs and make it harder to attract workers.
