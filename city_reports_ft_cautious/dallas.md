# Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington

**Grade: B+ (Above Average) | 63.3th percentile | November 2026**

---

The Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro area has an overall grade of B+, with a score of 63.3 out of 100. This city's economy is defined by strong job growth, with a 1.0% increase in employment over the past year. The city's weekly hours are also up 0.7%, indicating a genuine demand for labor.

**Labor Demand**
The city has 1.0% job growth, better than most metros. This means people looking for work have a good chance of finding a job.

**Unemployment**
The unemployment rate is 4.1%, about average for the 50 metros. This makes hiring neither very easy nor very hard for employers.

**Wage Growth**
Pay is growing 4.7% over the past year, faster than most metros. This means employers face rising labor costs, while workers see bigger paychecks.

**Cost of Living**
It takes 5.2 hours of pay to buy one square foot of home, priced at $197/sqft, down 2.5% from a year ago, with pay at $37.92/hr. This makes the city more affordable than most metros, attracting and keeping workers.

**Labor Force Growth**
The workforce is growing 0.6% over the past year, faster than most metros. This helps employers find new hires, as the labor pool is expanding.

**Building Permits**
New home approvals are down 8.4% over the past year. This signals a potential future housing supply squeeze, driving up prices.

**Days on Market**
Homes take 64 days to sell, about the same as a year ago. This indicates a softening housing market, making it harder for people to move here.

**Office Economy**
The city has a large office-based workforce, better than most metros. This suits businesses in tech, finance, and professional services, which thrive in this environment.

The city's biggest strength is its strong labor demand, driven by 1.0% job growth and rising weekly hours. However, its biggest risk is a potential housing supply squeeze, signaled by an 8.4% drop in new home approvals, which could drive up prices and make it harder for people to move here.
